Jopet PNG

In-country capability, resident teams.

Jopet Mining & Civil PNG Ltd is an IPA-incorporated company headquartered in Port Moresby, with resident teams and in-country parts inventory supporting mine operators and civil contractors across Papua New Guinea.

As the authorised dealer for Hitachi and Volvo Penta in PNG, we combine genuine equipment and parts with factory-trained technical services — and support PNG landowner companies through structured Hire-to-Buy programs.
